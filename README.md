# 🛡️ CrowdShield AI

### AI-Powered Crowd Flow Intelligence, Bottleneck Prediction & Dynamic Rerouting

> **Don't just react to crowds. Predict them, understand them, and move people intelligently.**

CrowdShield AI is an AI-powered crowd management platform designed for large venues such as stadiums, festivals, railway stations, concerts, and other high-density events.

The system combines **computer vision, anonymous people tracking, crowd-flow intelligence, venue simulation, graph-based routing, and dynamic rerouting** into one end-to-end platform.

---

## 🚨 Problem

Large venues can develop dangerous crowd bottlenecks around:

- Entry gates
- Security checkpoints
- Corridors
- Food counters
- Washrooms
- Seating areas
- Exits

The problem is not simply that "too many people are present."

The real problem is that crowd conditions can change quickly, while people are already moving through the venue.

CrowdShield AI aims to continuously understand:

- Where people are
- How many people are in each zone
- How quickly crowd density is increasing
- Where bottlenecks are developing
- Which routes are becoming risky
- Which alternative routes can distribute people more efficiently

---

# 💡 Solution
